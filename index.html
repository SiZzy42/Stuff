Frequently Asked Questions

How much gameplay do I need to record?
We recommend at least 30-60 minutes of clean gameplay for the AI to learn your unique aiming signature. The more data you provide, the more accurate the model becomes. Advanced users can record unlimited hours to train multiple models.
Will the AI exactly copy my playstyle?
Yes. The AI learns your exact aim patterns, reaction times, hesitations, and micro-corrections. When enabled, it aims identically to you—but faster and more consistent. This is actually safer because anti-cheat systems recognize it as your natural playstyle.
Is this undetectable?
Yes. Because the aimbot replicates YOUR unique aiming signature, anti-cheat systems see your natural behavior. Generic aimbots get caught because they use the same movement pattern for everyone. Your AI model is personal to you, making it invisible.
What's the difference between Basic and Pro?
Basic ($4.99): Full aimbot with Normal mode only. Gravity & reaction time humanization. All USB device support. Perfect for casual players. Cannot record or train AI models.

Pro ($14.99): Everything in Basic PLUS: unlimited recording, 3 custom AI models, Bezier + Silent + WindMouse modes, advanced fatigue sim, unlimited profiles, custom hotkeys, priority support, and beta features. Designed for competitive players.
Do I need special hardware?
No. XenoAI runs on any Windows 10/11 machine with a GPU (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Apple). We support CUDA and DirectML for maximum compatibility. A USB device (MAKCU, CH340, etc.) is recommended for 2-PC setup safety.
